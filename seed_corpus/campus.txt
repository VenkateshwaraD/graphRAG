Campus Guide

Turing Hall on North Campus contains the Department of Computer Science
main office and the Data Systems Research Lab. The main office is on the
third floor.

Maxwell Hall houses the Department of Mechanical Engineering and the
Sustainability Programs Office.

The Office of Student Services is located in Whitmore Student Center,
first floor. Whitmore Student Center also contains the campus bookstore,
which is operated under Policy LIB-7 shared services agreement with the
library system.

North Campus shuttle buses stop at Turing Hall every fifteen minutes
during term time.
