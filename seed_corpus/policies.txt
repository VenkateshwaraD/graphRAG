University Policies

Policy LIB-7 Library Circulation: late returns incur a fine of two
credits per day. Policy LIB-7 is administered by the Office of Student
Services.

Policy REG-3 Enrollment: students may register for a maximum of 18
credits per semester. Overrides require approval from the student's
department chair.

Policy EXAM-5 Examination Conduct: all midterm examinations are
scheduled by the Registrar's Office. The Registrar's Office reports to
the Vice Provost for Academic Affairs.

The Office of Student Services is a unit of the Division of Student
Affairs. The Division of Student Affairs is led by Dean Marcus Whitfield.
