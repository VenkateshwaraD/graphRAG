Faculty Directory

Dr. Priya Raman is a Professor in the Department of Computer Science.
She directs the Data Systems Research Lab and serves on the Graduate
Admissions Committee for the College of Engineering.

Dr. Alan Osei is a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science.
He is the faculty advisor for the Undergraduate Programming Club.

Dr. Lucia Ferreira is an Associate Professor in the Department of
Mechanical Engineering. She chairs the Sustainability Programs Office,
which oversees the Energy Systems Certificate.

The Department of Computer Science is housed in Turing Hall on North
Campus. The Department of Mechanical Engineering is in Maxwell Hall.
