Course Catalog — College of Engineering

CS-501 Advanced Database Systems is a graduate course offered by the
Department of Computer Science. The course is taught by Dr. Priya Raman.
Prerequisite for CS-501 is CS-301 Data Structures.

CS-301 Data Structures is an undergraduate course taught by
Dr. Alan Osei in the Department of Computer Science.

ME-210 Thermodynamics is taught by Dr. Lucia Ferreira in the Department
of Mechanical Engineering. ME-210 is a required course for the
Energy Systems Certificate.

The Energy Systems Certificate is administered by the Sustainability
Programs Office.
